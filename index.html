02 / ABOUT

Meet the artist

Filippos, performing as DEBOÖNE, is an emerging DJ and electronic music producer based in Greece.

His journey has spanned diverse electronic sounds — from a club residency to stepping out as an independent artist. His sound is rooted in Tech House, with Indie Dance and Melodic House influences running through it.

Bridging regional club scenes with the fast-paced nightlife of Athens, he lives by one rule: one time a DJ, always a DJ. A debut personal album is in development, alongside a steady stream of originals, immersive live sets and unrestricted studio creation.
